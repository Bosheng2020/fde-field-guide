# Contributing

Thank you for helping make this a more useful and accurate field guide.

## Useful contributions

- Corrections backed by a first-party source.
- Field-tested deployment patterns, including where they fail.
- Counterexamples that sharpen a framework or role boundary.
- Bilingual translation improvements that preserve meaning rather than word order.
- Templates or examples with sensitive and proprietary details removed.
- Regional perspectives on regulation, procurement, culture, and deployment practice.

## Evidence standard

When making claims about a named company, product, or role:

1. Prefer the organization’s official documentation, career page, engineering blog, paper, or repository.
2. Include the source URL and the date accessed.
3. Separate a direct claim from your inference.
4. Do not paste confidential material, customer data, or interview content covered by an NDA.

## Original frameworks

The FIELD Framework and FDE Delivery Maturity Model are attributed to Bosheng Ding. Improvements are welcome, but do not remove the originality note or present them as established industry standards.

## Pull requests

Keep each pull request focused. Explain:

- what changed;
- why it improves the guide;
- which sources or field evidence support it;
- whether both language versions were updated.
