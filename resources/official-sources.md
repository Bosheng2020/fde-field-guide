# Official FDE Sources / FDE 官方一手资料

**Last verified: 2026-10-22 / 最后核验：2026-10-22**

This page is a dated evidence map, not a universal job specification. It prioritizes official company career pages, product documentation, and company-authored material. Job postings are snapshots: titles, locations, travel requirements, and even URLs can change or disappear.

本页是带核验日期的证据地图，而不是一份放之四海而皆准的岗位说明。资料优先采用公司官方招聘页、产品文档与公司署名内容。招聘页面具有时效性：岗位名称、地点、出差比例乃至链接本身都可能调整或下线。

## Working definition / 工作定义

> **An FDE turns frontier capability into a reliable, adopted, measurable workflow—and turns field evidence back into product leverage.**
>
> **FDE 把前沿能力转化为可靠、被采用、可衡量的真实工作流，并把一线证据重新转化为产品与平台杠杆。**

Across the sources below, the role is broader than “customer support” and more hands-on than conventional advisory consulting. The recurring loop is: discover the real problem, scope an outcome, build inside the customer's constraints, deploy safely, drive adoption, measure results, and convert repeated field lessons into reusable product capability.

综合下列一手资料，FDE 不只是“客户支持”，也比传统顾问更强调亲手构建。反复出现的闭环是：发现真实问题、定义结果、在客户约束内构建、可靠上线、推动采用、衡量成效，再把重复出现的一线经验沉淀为可复用的产品能力。

## Origins and methodology / 起源与方法论

### Palantir

- Palantir says it “casually coined” several terms and job titles, including **Forward Deployed Engineers**, “decades ago.” This is a company-authored origin claim, not an independently established exact date or exclusive invention claim. Source: [Palantir Q2 2025 shareholder letter](https://www.palantir.com/q2-2025-letter/en).
- Palantir's Architecture Center describes the forward-deployed engineer as the **“human equivalent of backpropagation”**: a person close enough to the operating problem to synthesize field feedback with core engineering and ship improvements. Source: [Palantir Architecture Center overview](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/architecture-center/overview).
- A current official FDSE posting describes direct work with customers on open-ended, high-stakes problems; responsibilities span data architecture, application development, delivery, and executive communication. The cited Spain posting states **up to 25% travel**. Source: [Forward Deployed Software Engineer](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/53fb4c05-f949-4146-a046-5c063c36a628).

- Palantir 表示，公司在“几十年前”曾随意创造过包括 **Forward Deployed Engineers** 在内的若干术语与岗位名称。这是公司自身对起源的表述，不能据此断言精确年份或排他性的“发明权”。来源：[Palantir 2025 年第二季度股东信](https://www.palantir.com/q2-2025-letter/en)。
- Palantir 的 Architecture Center 把一线部署工程师形容为**“人类版本的反向传播”**：足够靠近真实问题，将一线反馈与核心工程结合，并推动产品改进落地。来源：[Palantir Architecture Center 概览](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/architecture-center/overview)。
- 当前官方 FDSE 招聘页强调：直接面对客户的开放式、高风险问题，工作横跨数据架构、应用开发、交付与高层沟通；所引西班牙岗位写明**最高 25% 出差**。来源：[Forward Deployed Software Engineer](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/53fb4c05-f949-4146-a046-5c063c36a628)。

## Cross-source competency model / 跨来源能力模型

1. **Outcome ownership / 结果负责** — Own a production and business result rather than a ticket queue. / 对生产结果和业务结果负责，而不是只完成需求单。
2. **Problem framing / 问题定义** — Turn ambiguous workflows into a bounded problem with users, constraints, baseline, acceptance criteria, and exit conditions. / 把模糊流程转化为包含用户、约束、基线、验收标准和退出条件的明确问题。
3. **End-to-end building / 端到端构建** — Deliver the vertical slice: model behavior, data, tools, permissions, UI, infrastructure, human fallback, and integration. / 交付完整纵向切片：模型行为、数据、工具、权限、界面、基础设施、人工兜底与系统集成。
4. **Production engineering / 生产工程** — Engineer reliability, observability, latency, cost, security, rollback, incident response, and handoff. / 处理可靠性、可观测性、时延、成本、安全、回滚、事故响应与交接。
5. **Evaluation / 评测** — Combine offline test sets, adversarial cases, online monitoring, human review, and business KPIs. / 结合离线测试集、对抗样例、线上监控、人工评审与业务 KPI。
6. **Adoption and change / 采用与变革** — Redesign workflow, train users, build trust, and remove organizational blockers. / 重塑工作流、培训用户、建立信任并清除组织障碍。
7. **Field-to-core learning / 一线到核心团队的学习闭环** — Convert customer evidence into model, product, platform, and documentation priorities. / 把客户证据转化为模型、产品、平台和文档的优先级。
8. **Reusable leverage / 可复用杠杆** — Generalize repeated delivery work into templates, components, tooling, and product improvements. / 把重复交付工作抽象为模板、组件、工具与产品改进。

## Evidence and lifecycle cautions / 证据与生命周期注意事项

- **Career pages are ephemeral.** Record the access date when quoting a posting, and save only what your organization's compliance policy permits. A dead link later does not prove the role never existed. / **招聘页具有时效性。** 引用时应记录访问日期，并仅按组织合规政策留存资料；链接日后失效不代表岗位从未存在。
- **Travel figures are posting-specific.** The percentages above are not industry-wide requirements and may differ by geography, seniority, account, or quarter. / **出差比例只适用于对应岗位。** 不应外推为行业标准；它可能因地区、级别、客户与季度而变化。
- **Origin claims require attribution.** Write “Palantir says…” rather than presenting the company's historical claim as independently verified fact. / **起源说法必须注明归属。** 应写“Palantir 表示……”，而不是把公司自己的历史叙述写成已被独立验证的事实。
- **Titles are not standards.** FDE, FDSE, deployment lead, and applied-AI roles overlap but are not interchangeable. Compare accountabilities, artifacts, and success metrics. / **岗位名称不是行业标准。** FDE、FDSE、Deployment Lead 与 Applied AI 岗位彼此重叠但并不等同，应比较责任边界、交付物和成功指标。
- **Recruiting copy is directional evidence.** It describes desired behavior, not audited proof that every team operates exactly that way. Pair it with interviews, references, engineering artifacts, and production evidence when making a hiring or career decision. / **招聘文案只是方向性证据。** 它说明期望行为，并不能审计式证明所有团队实际都如此运作。招聘或求职决策还应结合面试、背调、工程产物与生产证据。
