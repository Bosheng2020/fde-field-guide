## 8. Decision

- [ ] **Scale:** evidence supports more users, volume, workflows, or autonomy.
- [ ] **Maintain:** value is proven at the current scope; optimize operations.
- [ ] **Redesign:** value is plausible, but the system or workflow blocks adoption.
- [ ] **Hand off:** acceptance criteria are met and operational ownership is ready.
- [ ] **Stop:** expected value no longer justifies cost, risk, or opportunity cost.
